## A note sent with the submission

Submission guideline 3 lists `.bak`, `cache.bak` and `.lib.xml` among
the required files. None are generated by eSim 2.5 with KiCad 6:
KiCad 5 kept symbols in a separate cache library, KiCad 6 embeds them
in the schematic under `lib_symbols`. `.pro` and `.sch` appear as
`.proj` and `.kicad_sch` under current naming.

`README.txt` inside the zip sets this out, and the notification email
repeated it, so a reviewer checking the list mechanically would not
read the absence as an omission.
